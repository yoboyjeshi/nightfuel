# NightFuel

> Nights don't stop. Neither do you.

A better pick for the 2am vending machine — so you don't crash before dawn.

**Live site:** https://yoboyjeshi.github.io/nightfuel/

---

## The Problem

At 2am, the vending machine — or the servo on the way home — is your only option. No meal prep survives the night shift. So you stand there, grab the worst option, feel like garbage, and waste money. Every single shift.

## The Solution

NightFuel ranks what's actually in the machine by sugar, so you grab the pick that won't crash you before dawn. No typing. No scanning. No diet lecture. One number, one pick.

## Who it's for

Night-shift workers — the people whose entire food universe at 2am is a servo shelf and a vending machine.

*We help night-shift workers solve having only servo/vending junk at 2am by ranking what's in the machine by sugar.*

## Why I built this

I'm a night-shift Area Manager. Every shift, my break food is whatever the servo or the vending machine has — no prep, no time, and the worst options in front of me. I built NightFuel because it's the tool I wish I had: honest, no-BS, and straight to a better pick at 2am.
